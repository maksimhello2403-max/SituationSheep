Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

SituationSheep is a private diary of your romantic and sexual life. That is about as personal as data gets, so this page says plainly what happens to it — no shortcuts, no hedging.

Document 1 of 3 — Privacy Policy

The short version

  • Your entries are stored on our servers, encrypted in transit and on disk, and locked to your account so no other user can reach them. We won't tell you they never leave your phone — they do, so you can get them back when you lose it.
  • A very small number of our engineers can technically reach the database. Access is restricted, logged, and used only to fix faults — never to read your diary. We'd rather say this plainly than pretend it isn't true.
  • We don't sell anything and we don't run ads. No ad networks, no data brokers, no advertising trackers. Your entries are never used to train AI models.
  • We take nothing from your phone on our own. No contacts, no location, no calendar. Only what you type in.
  • Nothing is shared until you switch it on. Friends is off by default. Even switched on, friends see your numbers — never your names, notes, photos, places or dates.
  • You can use the app with no account at all, in which case your diary stays on your phone and we never receive it.
  • Delete means delete. One button in Settings clears your entries from this phone and from our servers.

3 Photos

A photo you attach to an entry is uploaded to our servers, in the same way as the rest of that entry, and is stored under your account where no other user can reach it. Before a photo leaves your device we strip its embedded metadata, including any GPS coordinates, so the place a photo was taken is not uploaded with it.

A face is the detail that makes another person identifiable. Nothing in the app requires a photo, and an entry works perfectly without one. If you would rather we never held a picture of someone, don't attach it.

4 Why we are allowed to hold it

  • Your diary entries, including anything about your private life or sexual health — your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR / UK GDPR), which you gave on a separate screen before you started, and which you can withdraw at any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • Your account and the running of the app — performance of our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Crash reports, usage statistics and marketing emails — your consent, given per item, each one off by default (Article 6(1)(a)).
  • Consent records and security logs — our legal obligation to demonstrate that consent was obtained (Article 7(1)), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure (Article 6(1)(f)).

Withdrawing consent is exactly as easy as giving it: one tap in Settings, no email to support and no form to fill in. Withdrawal does not undo processing that was lawful before it.

5 What we never do

  • We never sell your personal information, and we have not done so in the preceding twelve months.
  • We never share it with advertisers, ad networks, or data brokers. There are no advertising or attribution trackers in this app.
  • We never use your entries to train AI models.
  • We never use your entries for marketing, profiling, or automated decision-making.

If any of that ever changes, we will ask you again on a fresh consent screen. We will not update this page quietly and treat your continued use as agreement.

6 The people you write about

A note about someone is information about another person who never agreed to any of this. Two things follow.

What we ask of you. Keep them unidentifiable. A first name or a nickname is enough. Leave out surnames, phone numbers, profile links, addresses, and anything about their health. Think twice before attaching a photo.

What we do. We hold that information only to show it back to you. We never index it, search it across users, disclose it, or use it for any purpose of our own. We do not notify the people described in your diary, because contacting someone to tell them they appear in another person's private records would cause exactly the harm this policy exists to prevent — a disproportionate effort exemption under Article 14(5)(b) GDPR. If a person believes their information is held here, they can contact us at maksim.hello2403@gmail.com.

7 Who else sees it

Other users — only if you switch Friends on. Friends is off by default. If you turn it on, other users can find you by your nickname, and the people you accept can see your counts: partners, situationships, relationships, countries. They never see names, notes, photos, places or dates. You can switch it off and remove anyone at any time.

Our hosting provider. Google Cloud Platform / Firebase (Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited) stores and processes the data on our behalf as our processor, under a data processing agreement. They do not use it for their own purposes.

Nobody else, unless we are legally compelled to. If we receive a valid legal demand for your data we will tell you, unless we are legally forbidden from doing so.

8 Where it is kept

Your data is stored on Google Cloud Platform infrastructure, operated by Google on our behalf. Where data is transferred outside the UK or the European Economic Area, that transfer is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses together with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, plus additional technical measures including encryption in transit and at rest.

9 How long we keep it

  • Diary entries, photos and counts — until you delete them, or until you delete your account, whichever comes first.
  • Account details — until you delete your account.
  • Deleted data — removed from live systems immediately and purged from encrypted backups within 30 days.
  • Consent records — kept for 3 years after account deletion, because we are required to be able to demonstrate that consent was given and withdrawn. These contain nothing from your diary.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics — 90 days.

10 How it is protected

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access rules on our database restrict every record to the account that owns it, so no user can read another user's entries. Administrative access to production is restricted to a small number of engineers, requires multi-factor authentication, and is logged.

What we will not claim. This app does not use end-to-end encryption. Your entries are stored in a form we could technically read. We don't, our access is restricted and logged, and reading a user's diary would be a firing offence — but we are not going to promise you something the architecture doesn't deliver.

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

This section applies to residents of Washington State under the My Health My Data Act, and to residents of Nevada under SB 370. It sits alongside the Privacy Policy above and does not replace it.

A What consumer health data we collect

Information you enter in your diary that relates to your sexual activity, your relationships, and any health-related detail you choose to record about an encounter — including a rating, a description, a date and a region.

B Where it comes from

From you, and only from you. We collect no consumer health data from any other source — not from other apps, not from data brokers, not from your device's health services, not by inference.

C Why we collect it

To run your personal diary and, if you have switched on backup, to sync it across your devices. That is the only purpose. We do not use it for advertising, marketing, profiling, research, or model training.

D Who we share it with

Google Cloud Platform / Firebase, as our hosting processor, which stores it on our behalf and may not use it for its own purposes. Nobody else. We have never sold consumer health data and the app is built so that we cannot: selling it would require your separate signed authorisation, and we do not offer one.

E Your rights under the My Health My Data Act

  • Confirm whether we hold your consumer health data, and access it — Settings → Privacy → Export everything.
  • Get a list of every third party we have shared it with — write to maksim.hello2403@gmail.com.
  • Withdraw consent to its collection and sharing — Settings → Privacy.
  • Delete it — Settings → Privacy → Delete account and all data. We will delete it from live systems, backups and any processor within 30 days of your request.

We will not deny you the app, charge you differently, or give you a worse version for exercising any of these. If we refuse a request you may appeal to maksim.hello2403@gmail.com, and if we deny the appeal you may complain to the Washington State Attorney General at atg.wa.gov.
